I run a small business with 6 employees all on Vodafone. What's the best way to protect against phishing and SIM-swap attacks? Should I be looking at enterprise MDM solutions or is there something simpler?

Great question, Sarah. For a 6-person business, you're in a useful middle ground — complex enough to need real protection, but small enough that enterprise MDM may be overkill.

For phishing protection: Microsoft Defender for Business (£2.50/user/month) or Google Workspace's built-in protections are well-suited. Both include email scanning, safe link checking and admin controls.

For SIM-swap specifically: Ask Vodafone to add a port-freeze or verbal passcode to your account — this stops number transfers without in-person verification. This is a free, underused protection.

On MDM: Microsoft Intune is robust but complex. For 6 devices, consider Jamf Now (Apple) or Scalefusion — both have straightforward setup and are genuinely sized for small teams.

S
Does Vodafone offer any business security bundles that would cover this?

That's a great question to send back to VodafoneGPT — it has direct access to your account and Vodafone's current business security portfolio, so it'll give you accurate, up-to-date bundle options and pricing specific to your account.
